# PaperLike: Product Brief

PaperLike is a free, local-first EPUB reader for Windows and macOS. The goal is to make reading more comfortable and enjoyable with a calm paper-like page and fewer steps.

## First version

- Import EPUBs into a local library while leaving originals untouched.
- Restore each book's last-read chapter.
- Paper, Ivory, and Night page tones with adjustable text size.
- Installable macOS app and Windows app, with per-user data folders.
- No account, cloud sync, storefront, subscriptions, or monetization.

## Current prototype limits

The reader extracts chapter text. It does not yet render book images or rich styling and does not include a contents panel, bookmarks, search, PDF, or exact scroll-position restore. Its current source license has not been selected.
