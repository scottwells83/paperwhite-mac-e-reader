# Product Brief: PaperLike

## Goal

A free, personal e-reader for Windows and macOS that makes reading feel calm and comfortable, with warm paper tones and as few steps as possible.

## Confirmed direction

- EPUB is the first format.
- Keep books and progress on the user's computer; copy imported files into PaperLike's library without changing the original.
- Use a simple reading room with Paper, Ivory, and Night tones, adjustable text size, chapter navigation, and saved chapter position.
- Avoid accounts, cloud sync, store, subscription, or monetization flows.
- Kindle is a functional reference, not a branding target.

## Installation and storage

- macOS: Apple Silicon and Intel disk images; per-user app under `~/Applications/PaperLike.app`; library under `~/Library/Application Support/PaperLike/`.
- Windows: x64 portable preview; per-user setup executable configured to build through GitHub Actions after the source repository is pushed.
- The local Mac packages are ad-hoc signed and not notarized.

## Current limits

Chapter text is supported. EPUB images, rich styling, contents navigation, bookmarks, search, PDF, and exact scroll-position resume are not implemented. The source license and GitHub destination are undecided.
